# Project conventions

## Terminology

Use **ACO** instead of **THN** in project documentation, product copy, and explanations. This is an explicit user terminology decision. Avoid duplicating the term as “ACO ACO”.

Use **member** instead of **patient** for the individual in project documentation, product copy, proposed field names, and explanations. Interpret user references to a patient as a member unless they explicitly distinguish the terms. Preserve externally defined source identifiers and quotations when accuracy requires the original wording, mapping them to member terminology in the product.

## Requirements and user definitions

Keep approved workflow labels and meanings captured in `docs/definitions.md` and consistent with `docs/provisional-input-contract.md`, the README, and the eventual product/user guide. Mark unapproved rules as pending. Definitions must distinguish outreach outcomes, work priority, follow-up categories, and evidence-based funnel milestones. Continue requirements review one decision at a time and wait for the user's confirmation before advancing.
